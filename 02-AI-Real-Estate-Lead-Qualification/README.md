# 🏠 AI Real Estate Lead Qualification & Scoring System

An AI-powered Real Estate Lead Qualification and Scoring automation system built with **n8n, AI Agents, OpenAI, Google Sheets, Google Calendar, Telegram, Gmail, and JavaScript**.

This project automates the process of collecting, validating, analyzing, scoring, storing, and routing real estate leads to the appropriate sales workflow.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

Real estate businesses receive leads from different sources, but manually checking every lead can take time.

This automation system processes incoming real estate leads and automatically:

- Collects lead information
- Cleans and normalizes customer data
- Validates phone numbers and email addresses
- Checks for duplicate leads
- Generates sequential Lead IDs
- Uses an AI Agent to analyze the lead
- Generates a Lead Score
- Classifies leads as Hot, Warm, or Cold
- Stores lead information in Google Sheets
- Creates calendar events for qualified leads
- Sends Telegram notifications
- Sends Hot Lead notifications to the sales team through Gmail

The goal is to reduce manual lead-processing work and help sales teams organize and respond to leads more efficiently.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to build an automated AI-powered lead qualification system for real estate businesses.

The system converts raw lead information into structured and actionable sales data.
